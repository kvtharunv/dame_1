Near Real-Time Remote Sensing via Google Earth Engine

Cloud-penetrating Sentinel-1 C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) VV/VH automated water detection pipeline (< -16.5 dB backscatter threshold).

Radar Remote Sensing Architecture

During severe monsoon dam break events, optical satellites (Landsat, Sentinel-2) are blinded by 100% cloud cover. Sentinel-1 SAR C-band radar penetrates dense clouds, rain, and operates 24/7 day and night.

Specular Reflection Mechanism: Smooth standing flood water reflects radar pulses away from satellite, registering backscatter values below -16.5 dB.
Automatic Baseline Masking: Permanent river channels are automatically subtracted from pre-flood reference scenes to isolate newly flooded regions.
SRTM DEM Slope Filter: Eliminates radar mountain terrain shadow artifacts by masking out slopes > 5°.

Project Explained in Simple Everyday English (Easy to Read & Explain)

READ THIS FIRST

What is this project in one simple sentence?
This project is a computer simulation tool that shows on Google Maps what happens if a dam breaks or a river gets blocked in India, showing exactly where the water will rush, how fast it will travel, which areas are in extreme danger, which areas have mild flooding, and where people can safely run to survive.

The 3 Zones You See During Simulation:

  • 🔴 High Danger Area (Red Zone): Deep water (> 2.5 meters) rushing violently at high speed (> 5 m/s). Destroys houses, roads, and bridges. Zero survival if trapped. Must evacuate before water arrives!
  • 🟡 Low Danger Area (Yellow/Amber Zone): Shallow flood water (0.3 to 2.5 meters) spreading into farmlands and roads. People can still walk or drive to high ground.
  • 🟢 Safe Places / Havens (Green Shields): High hills, elevated schools, and disaster shelters built 25 to 600 meters above the valley floor. 100% safe from flood water.

Continuous Flow & Realistic Dam Sizing:

  • Width Scaled to Dam Breach: The flood starts narrow at the dam crest (calibrated to the physical breach size) and widens naturally as it travels downstream.
  • Continuous Flow: Water moves smoothly along every curve of the river without jumping or flickering.
  • Never Disappears: When the simulation reaches 6 hours, the full maximum flood extent remains permanently visible on screen.
  • Relaxed Speed & Replay: Runs at 0.25x (or 0.1x ultra-slow) and offers a 1-click Replay button.

1. Platform Executive Summary & Disaster Context

HYDRO-BREAK 360 is an automated hydrodynamic modeling, spaceborne radar early warning, and Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) scenario-generation web platform. Built specifically for the National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) Problem Statement 26161 (Smart India Hackathon 2026), it bridges the gap between high-performance computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and frontline emergency rescue deployment.

Chamoli Disaster (Feb 2021 Precedent): A massive rock and hanging glacier detachment in the Nanda Devi sanctuary dammed the Rishi Ganga and catastrophically breached, releasing high-velocity debris torrents down the Dhauliganga river that destroyed the Raini bridge and submerged the Tapovan Vishnugad tunnels.
Kosi River Breach (Aug 2008 Precedent): Catastrophic avulsion at Kusaha (Indo-Nepal border) shifted the river course 100+ km eastward across North Bihar flatlands, inundating over 2.7 million people and demonstrating the need for regional overbank storage modeling.
Operational Mission: Predict flood wave celerity, breach hydrograph discharge $Q(t)$, village-by-village arrival deadlines, and delineate High Danger Areas, Low Danger Areas, and High-Ground Safe Places in real time.

A modular, full-stack architecture combining high-performance scientific Python on the backend with responsive, hardware-accelerated mapping on the frontend:

Architecture Layer Language / Runtime Library / Tool Functional Purpose & Implementation Details

3. Multi-Tier Hazard Classification & Safety Indication System

During hydrodynamic simulations, the platform dynamically delineates three distinct levels of spatial safety and operational risk:

🔴 HIGH DANGER AREA Depth ≥ 2.5 m · Vel ≥ 5.0 m/s Primary surge torrent corridor. Catastrophic hydrodynamic pressure destroys masonry, washes out bridges, and creates zero-survival conditions. Total evacuation deadline expires upon front arrival.
🟡 LOW DANGER AREA Depth 0.3 m – 2.5 m Peripheral floodplain diffusion & backwater storage. Submerges agricultural crop fields, causes road waterlogging, and cuts off low-lying evacuation routes. Advisory warning active.
🟢 SAFE PLACES / HAVENS Elevation > +25 m to +600 m Designated high-ground disaster relief camps, hospital plateaus, high-school mounds, and elevated military bases located 100% outside and above the peak flood surge reach.

4. Summary of Web Pages & Interface Workspaces

Tab 1: Flow Simulation Map (tab-map): Fullscreen GIS workspace. Renders dam breach crest origin, Google Maps satellite tiles, Lagrangian water particles, High & Low Danger polygon zones, Green Safe Haven pins, live floating telemetry HUD, and bottom timeline playback controller.
Tab 2: SPH vs Delft3D Comparison (tab-compare): Dual-solver hydrodynamic evaluation workspace. Pits Lagrangian particle mechanics against Eulerian 2D shallow water equations, showing peak near-field velocities, arrival lead times, and station-by-station physics discrepancies.
Tab 3: Hydrograph & Longitudinal Profile (tab-hydrograph): Hydraulic source engineering workspace. Features the Froehlich (2008) ASCE outflow hydrograph $Q(t)$ ($Q_p$, $t_f$, $B_{\text{avg}}$) and the longitudinal valley elevation profile showing riverbed drop and water surface slopes.
Tab 4: HADR Loss Assessment (tab-hadr): Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief decision support dashboard. Displays exposed population, critical infrastructure at risk, crop acreage loss in hectares, INR Crore financial damage, and sector-by-sector evacuation countdown timers.
Tab 5: GEE Sentinel-1 SAR Remote Sensing (tab-gee): Spaceborne radar analysis module. Outlines cloud-penetrating Sentinel-1 C-band SAR specular reflection physics ($< -16.5\text{ dB}$), enables map overlays, and supplies a copy-ready Google Earth Engine Python script.
Tab 6: GIS Export Deliverables (tab-export): Data hub providing one-click browser downloads of ESRI Shapefile packages (.zip), Google Earth KML (.kml), GeoTIFF depth rasters (.tif), and structured HADR operational JSON reports.
Tab 7: About System & Operational Guide (tab-about): This comprehensive reference manual detailing the system methods, technologies, navigation bars, and hazard classifications.
